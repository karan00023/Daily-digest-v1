Oil falls about 7%, its biggest drop since July, as the US and Iran signal room for diplomacy

WTI is back below $100. Rubio says no decision on new strikes has been made.

Brent settled near $104.77 and WTI near $96.46 on Monday, each down about 7%. Reports said Trump told Netanyahu he wants to let talks play out, and Secretary of State Rubio said Trump prefers a diplomatic resolution. Iran's foreign minister said negotiators in Qatar are working on a proposal that could reopen the Strait of Hormuz within days if conditions are met, including ending the US blockade and lifting oil sanctions.

Why this matters to me

Cheaper oil helps on inflation, rates and airfares, and with the rupee. It would also take some of the geopolitical bid out of gold (GLD). Treat this as a positive but fragile development.

News AVGO relevance 6.3

Marvell agrees to buy Lumentum in an all-stock deal valuing it near $115B, and Marvell shares fall 12.7%

Optical networking is becoming the next bottleneck in AI data centres.

Marvell will pay about a 24% premium for Lumentum, a maker of optical components used in AI data-centre networking. Investors balked at the price. Lumentum was trading at about a fifth of that valuation a year ago, and Marvell closed at $171.41.

Read more · 2 sources

Why it matters

Chipmakers are buying their way into the optical interconnects that link AI clusters, a market where Broadcom competes.

Why this matters to me

Relevant to AVGO's networking franchise, and a sign the AI build-out is shifting from GPUs to the plumbing between them.

How I could use this

For infrastructure planning, data-centre network bandwidth (not just compute) is increasingly the cost and lead-time constraint to model.
