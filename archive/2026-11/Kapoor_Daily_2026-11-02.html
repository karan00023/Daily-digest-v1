News relevance 7.5

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft and Anthropic agree to honour one robots.txt signal for opting out of AI training

A single token, __usage_ai-training. Google's version also affects grounding in Gemini and Vertex AI.

Announced on 30 October, the voluntary standard lets publishers block AI training with one robots.txt entry. Anthropic has reportedly adopted it already, while Google and Microsoft plan support in 2027 and OpenAI has yet to publish details. Microsoft also introduced a separate "AI Grounding" setting that controls whether pages can feed answers in Bing, Copilot and Microsoft 365. Meta hasn't committed.

Read more · 2 sources

Why it matters

Training and grounding are now separate controls, which data-governance teams have long asked for.

Why this matters to me

If your company publishes documentation or data externally, there's now a decision to make about training versus grounding.

How I could use this

Draft a one-page policy note for your web and data teams: which public content to keep available for grounding (search answers, Copilot) but exclude from training, and the exact robots.txt entries for each vendor.

LinkedIn angle

“Training versus grounding: the AI content control enterprises actually needed.” A practical robots.txt guide.
