Lead: Core PCE inflation hits a three-year high of 3.3%, above forecasts; stocks recover from a 3% intraday plunge to close mixed before Thanksgiving

The Fed's preferred inflation gauge rose 0.3% in October. Core PCE (excluding food and energy) rose 3.3% from a year earlier, up from 3.1% and above the 3.2% consensus, the highest in three years. Headline PCE rose to 3.8%. It was the last major inflation reading before the Fed's 9 December decision. Stocks opened sharply lower on inflation and AI worries, then recovered most of the losses: the Dow rose 0.33%, while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq ended modestly lower. US markets are closed today and close at 1 p.m. on Friday.
