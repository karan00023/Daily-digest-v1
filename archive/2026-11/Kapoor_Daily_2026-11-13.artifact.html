Jensen Huang resigns as Nvidia CEO after 33 years; CFO Colette Kress is named interim CEO

It's effective immediately, five days before Nvidia's earnings. The stock fell as much as 13% before closing down 4.3%.

Nvidia's 8-K says Colette Kress, executive vice president and CFO, will serve as interim CEO while keeping her CFO role until a permanent successor is named. The board is considering internal and external candidates. The departure followed the board's investigation, opened on 8 November, into reports that Huang was targeted by a foreign intelligence effort. Nvidia gave no reason in its announcement. The broader market recovered most of its early losses.

Why this matters to me

For your AMD and AVGO positions, analysts are asking whether Nvidia's turmoil sends business to rivals. Yahoo's headline said AMD and Broadcom rose on Thursday. Nvidia's report on Tuesday is now the event of the month for your chip exposure.
