AI chip meltdown: S&P 500 falls 4.4%, its worst day since April 2025, and the chip index plunges 12%

Fears of AI and data-centre regulation under a Democratic Congress wiped out Wednesday's rally and more.

The Nasdaq fell 5.52% into correction territory and the Dow lost 1,833 points. AMD closed down 17.3% at $404.40 despite Tuesday's strong report, Broadcom fell 10.4% to $291.53, and Nvidia fell 9.3%. The PHLX Semiconductor Index dropped 12.25%. Reuters tied the selloff to worries about tougher AI rules and slower data-centre construction, while weak outlooks from Qualcomm and Intel added to doubts about chip demand. Nasdaq futures were about 1% higher early Friday.

Why this matters to me

Your two largest AI hardware holdings, AMD and AVGO, took the biggest hits, and GOOGL, MSFT and ORCL are exposed to the same theme. Prices like these test whether your plan holds. Look back at what you wrote down before AMD's report rather than reacting today.

Why it matters

This is a policy-driven repricing, not a change in demand. AMD's data-center business nearly doubled two days earlier. The market is pricing the risk that Washington slows the AI build-out. Traders now see a December Fed hike as more than 65% likely, according to one report.
